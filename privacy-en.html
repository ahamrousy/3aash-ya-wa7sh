Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who collects the data?

Dr. Ahmed Amrousy, founder of 3aash Ya Wa7sh, is responsible for your data. The only other people who can see it are the coaches working on your own program, and only as much as they need to design and follow it up.

What exactly is collected?

Only what you type into the form: your name, WhatsApp number, your email and city if you give them, your sporting objectives, your age, gender, weight, height and waist measurement, your current activity, your availability, and your answers to the health safety questions. Nothing is collected behind your back.

What is it used for?

One purpose only: so our coaches can design a training program that suits you and follow your progress on it. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with any company or person, and not published.

Where is it stored?

In a private, locked Google Sheet owned by Dr. Ahmed Amrousy. It is not public, and nobody outside the 3aash Ya Wa7sh team can reach it. This website stores nothing on a server of its own, and these pages contain no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel and no tracking tool of any kind.

How long is it kept?

For as long as you are in the program, plus one year afterwards, so your history is there if you come back. After that it is deleted — and you can ask for deletion at any time before that.

Your rights

Under Egypt’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 you have the right to: know what data is held about you, correct anything wrong, withdraw your consent at any time, and ask for your data to be deleted entirely. Withdrawing consent or deleting your data means we stop following up your program, but has no other consequence.

How to ask for correction or deletion

Send a message to the WhatsApp number or email below with your name and your 3aash Ya Wa7sh ID (AYW-...). We will action it within 7 days at the latest. Deleting your data also deletes your account, your program and everything you logged.

Children

If you are under 18, the form is not accepted without a guardian’s name, number and explicit consent.

Photos and content

We never show your photo, your story or your numbers in any content unless you explicitly agreed to it, and we ask you again before each time.

Your account

After you send the form you get an account. Your username is your mobile number, and you choose your own password. We store the password only in scrambled (hashed) form, so nobody can read it — not even us. If you forget it, the coach sends you a link to set a new one.

Your program and what you log

We store your program and what you log against it: the sessions you did, your effort, your notes and your weekly check-ins (weight, waist and energy). Only the coaching team sees it, to follow you up and adjust your program.

Staying logged in on your device

Your browser keeps a login code so you stay signed in for up to 30 days. When you log out, the code is deleted and stops working. There are no other cookies and no tracking.

Events you save

If you save an event on the Find Your Challenge page, we store that you saved it, linked to your account, so you can find it again. Registering for the event happens on the organiser’s site, and we never receive that registration data.
