What is 3aash Ya Wa7sh?

3aash Ya Wa7sh is a free community initiative in Egypt, founded by Dr. Ahmed Amrousy — an engineer and endurance athlete, not a coach. It helps people who do not exercise take their first steps towards a regularly active life.

You fill in a 6-step form, and professional coaches write an exercise program around your goal, schedule and health. You track your sessions in your account on the site, and when you are ready you can find a race or sports challenge in Egypt and the Gulf on Find Your Challenge.

In short

  • A free community initiative in Egypt — not a gym or a coaching business.
  • Built for complete beginners, and open to anyone aged 12 and up.
  • Exercise programs are written by professional coaches — never generated automatically.
  • The founder, Dr. Ahmed Amrousy, is an engineer and endurance athlete, not a coach.
  • Find Your Challenge lists races and sports events in Egypt and the Gulf, with their official links.

Common questions

Who can join?

Anyone aged 12 and up, at any level. Under 18s need a guardian’s name and consent.

Do I need to be fit already?

Not at all. Your program starts from where you are today, even if you have never trained.

Will I train at home or in a gym?

Whatever you have. The form asks about gym, pool, bike and outdoors, and the program is built on that.

When do I get my program?

Our team reviews every form personally; your program reaches your account and WhatsApp within a few days.

Is the program generated automatically?

No. Every program is written and checked by a coach; nothing reaches you without a person seeing it.

I have a health condition — can I join?

Yes. The safety questions tell us, and if needed we ask for your doctor’s OK before you start, then build a plan that suits you.

Do you push supplements or hormones?

No hormones or steroids, ever. Supplements are not required — the base is normal balanced food and regular training.

How do you follow me up?

In your account you tick each session and log your weight weekly; the coach sees it and messages you on WhatsApp if anything needs changing.

Will my photos or results be published?

Never without your explicit consent, and we ask again each time.

Can I pause if life gets in the way?

Yes. Message the coach on WhatsApp; we pause your program and pick up when you are back.

Our promise to you

  • A real coach writes and follows your program — not a machine.
  • No hormones or steroids, ever.
  • Safety before speed: a doctor’s OK whenever it is needed.
  • Your data and photos stay with us and are never shared without your permission.
  • Realistic goals we agree together, and a program that adapts to your progress.
