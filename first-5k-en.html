Your first 5K: a beginner running plan

By the 3aash Ya Wa7sh team · General guidance, not a substitute for a doctor’s advice or a coach’s program.

Short answer: start with run-walk intervals — one minute of running and two of walking, eight times — three days a week, and lengthen the running a little each week. Many people reach 30 minutes of continuous running (close to 5 km) in about eight weeks. If a week is hard, repeat it.

5 km is a great first race: close enough to reach in two months, big enough to be proud of. Nobody will look twice if you walk for part of it — that is completely normal.

Before you run

  • If you are not active at all yet, walk briskly for two weeks first (see how to start exercising from zero).
  • Your health: if you have a heart, blood-pressure, blood-sugar or joint problem, ask your doctor before you start running.
  • Shoes: comfortable running shoes in your size matter more than anything else. They need not be expensive.
  • Warm-up: start every session with five minutes of brisk walking and finish with five minutes of easy walking.

The plan: 8 weeks, 3 sessions a week

Leave a rest day or an easy walk between sessions. “Run” here means a very easy jog — a pace at which you can still talk.

At an easy beginner pace, 5 km takes many people somewhere around 30–45 minutes. Your time does not matter in a first race — finishing happy does.

Running in Egypt

  • Heat: run early in the morning or in the evening, drink water before and after, and slow down on very hot days.
  • Where: a club or youth-centre track, a compound, or a quiet street with a pavement. Stay away from heavy traffic.
  • Safety: run in well-lit places with people around, and let someone know where you are.
  • Pain: some muscle tiredness is normal. Sharp pain, joint pain, or pain that grows as you run means stop and get advice.

How to choose your first race

  1. Pick a 5K at least 8–10 weeks from today.
  2. Close to home, so travel does not wear you out the first time.
  3. Marked beginner-friendly, or with a short distance alongside the long ones.
  4. Register only on the organiser’s official site, and check the price and date there.

Common questions

How do I start running as a complete beginner?

Start with run-walk intervals: one minute running and two walking, eight times, three days a week, after a five-minute brisk walk. Lengthen the running a little each week. If you are not active at all yet, walk for two weeks first.

How do I train for my first 5K?

Pick a race at least 8–10 weeks away and follow a run-walk plan three times a week until you can run 25–30 minutes non-stop. Ease off in the final week, sleep well, and do not try new shoes or new food on race day.

How do I choose the right sports challenge for me?

Choose for your level today, not the level you wish you had: a short distance, close to home, two or more months away, and marked beginner-friendly. Find Your Challenge filters events by level, place and date.
